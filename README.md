### 🔬 Research Interests
- **GeoAI & Knowledge-Guided Deep Learning**: PyTorch U-Net ensembles, mass-conserving pycnophylactic constraints, and spatial transferability.
- **Human Mobility Downscaling**: Fusing multi-source environmental covariates (~5.5 km → 100 m) with WorldPop and Call Detail Records (CDR) for disease modeling.
- **Spatial Epidemiology & Precision Public Health**: Accurate Human-Vector Contact Zone (HVCZ) prediction for precision malaria modeling in Zambia (NSF #2451156).
- **Satellite Remote Sensing**: Planetary-scale Earth observation on Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-2, Landsat, and ecological health indices (RSEI).
- **Transportation Econometrics & Land Growth**: 2SLS instrumental variables and spatial regression of induced travel demand and highway expansion (TDOT/FHWA).
